At ICHS, bright fixtures illuminate the ceiling and some bulbs are exposed to my view. These are my firsthand comfort observations.

Firsthand comparison: daylight and eye comfort

Both clinics’ patient rooms were on the second floor. I found ICHS exam-room lighting so bright that I wore tinted lenses over my regular glasses. I observed smaller windows and closed blinds at ICHS, compared with large windows and open blinds at SIEAM, where daylight was the primary light source during my visit. SIEAM was comfortable enough for me to remove my tinted lenses to take photos.

The ICHS photos include a specimen/task room with blinds partly lowered; the closed-blind observation refers to my exam-room experience. No exam-room lux readings were supplied for either clinic. My comfort observations describe my experience and are not inferred from camera exposure.
